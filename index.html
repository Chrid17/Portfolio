Who I Am

I'm a Software Developer and Data Scientist based in Windhoek, Namibia. I graduated with a Bachelor of Computer Science (Software Engineering) from the Namibia University of Science & Technology in May 2026 and I'm now in the final semester of my Data Science Honours (NQF Level 8), researching road-accident hotspots in Windhoek.

I also support clients on Odoo ERP, where I'm certified.

I'm committed to excellence, teamwork, and continuous learning, with a strong drive to build technologies that contribute to positive change and digital growth.
